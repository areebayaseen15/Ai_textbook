
# Specifications for the `Resources` 

The `Resources`  contains additional reference materials that complement the core content of the **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics** textbook. These files provide extended learning resources, terminology explanations, real-world use cases, and practical guidance to support students throughout the course.

---

## Purpose of the Extras Module

The primary goal of this module is to offer **supportive, non-chapter content** that enhances the student's understanding of:

- Real-world applications of Physical AI  
- Important technical terms and definitions  
- Recommended references for further study  
- Best practices for simulations, hardware, and robot development  

These resources help students develop a deeper and more practical understanding of the concepts taught in the main chapters.

---

## Files Included in the Module

### **1. real-world-applications.md**
This file explores **how Physical AI and humanoid robotics are used in industries**, research labs, healthcare, service robotics, disaster response, and entertainment.  
It helps students connect course theory to **practical real-world systems**.

---

### **2. glossary.md**
A detailed glossary covering the **key technical terms** used throughout the course, including:  
- AI  
- Embodied Intelligence  
- ROS 2  
- SLAM  
- Digital Twin  
- URDF  
and more.  
This serves as a quick reference for students while reading chapters or working on labs.

---

### **3. references.md**
A curated list of recommended **books, research papers, documentation sites, tutorials, and open-source repositories** related to:  
- ROS 2  
- Isaac Sim  
- Unity  
- Humanoid robotics  
- Embodied AI  
This helps students dive deeper into advanced topics.

---

### **4. tips-best-practices.md**
This document includes practical advice for:  
- Working in simulation  
- Handling hardware safely  
- Managing code and ROS nodes  
- Planning capstone projects  
- Collaborating effectively  

It is especially helpful during labs and final project development.

---

## Why These Files Were Created

These files were created to:

- Extend the learning experience beyond the standard textbook chapters  
- Provide reference material commonly needed while working with robotics  
- Support students during assignments, simulations, and capstone projects  
- Offer guidance on tools, platforms, and best practices  
- Ensure the course remains practical, relevant, and application-driven  

---

## How Students Should Use the Extras Folder

Students can use the extras module to:

- Look up technical terms during reading  
- Explore industry applications for inspiration  
- Find high-quality external resources for research  
- Follow recommended best practices during hands-on work  
- Strengthen understanding of Physical AI & robotics concepts  

---

### Summary

The `extras` folder acts as a **support pack** for the textbook—helping students gain clarity, inspiration, and practical insights while progressing through the course.
